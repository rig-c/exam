## 3. What Could Not Be Fixed and Why

- **Every row of the pass table was fixed (`14/14 PASS`, 0 fail, 0 skip).**
- **Remaining non-graded 2D rig limitations:**
  1. **Extreme shin foreshortening in flight (`LEG-SCALE` clamped at `0.5`):** When the runner kicks her heel back behind the thigh during flight (`frames 6–14` on `leg_r1` and `frames 18–23` on `leg_l1`), the 2D projected shin length drops as low as `0.089–0.094` of straight length. Clamping `--leg-scale` at `0.5,1.0` avoids crushing the shin region attachment into a sliver while still lifting both feet well clear of the floor (`+0.0456` leg lengths minimum clearance) and keeping median lift ratio at `1.116`. Depicting a fully folded back-kick without `scalex` compression would require an alternate drawing set (`alternates`), which this rig does not ship with.
  2. **Dropped `idle` tracks (`IDLE-DROPPED`):** The rig's `idle` animation has a period of `3.333333s` (100 frames at 30 fps), which is 5 running cycles rather than 1 running cycle (20 frames = `0.666667s`), and includes `chest_ctl` `translatey`. Leaving `--carry-idle` off keeps the 20-frame running cycle strictly periodic between cycle 1 and cycle 10 (`loop` position difference `0.0000` leg lengths).

## 4. What the Tools Should Have Done

1. **`rigc render` (`README.md`):** `node_modules/rig-c/README.md` (line 429) states that `rigc render` writes `render/<animation>/f0000.png…`, omitting the fact that passing a non-default `--fps <n>` appends `@<fps>fps` to the output directory (`render/<animation>@<fps>fps/`). `README.md` should state the `@<fps>fps` directory naming alongside the `--fps` flag description, matching `docs/AUTHORING.md` §0.3.
2. **`rig-pose retarget` (`--plant-feet` off on `"travel": "in-place"` sequences):** When run without `--plant-feet` on a sequence declared `"travel": "in-place"`, `rig-pose retarget` still applies the non-in-place step-release rule (`r 1–199 -> the pose's travel, never re-planted`) when recording `feet.supports` in `report.json`, fragmenting support spans into single-frame intervals (`[0,0]`, `[2,2]`, ...) so `tools/grade.py`'s `slide` row prints `SKIP` (`no two consecutive grounded frames with sequence ankles`) instead of measuring the unplanted foot slide. `supportsOf` should respect `"travel": "in-place"` when reading support spans even when `--plant-feet` is off (or `grade.py` should fall back to grounded frames outside `feet.jumps`).
3. **`rig-pose retarget` (`SCALE` diagnostic vs. leg and hip-width ratios):** When `translate.scale` (e.g., `mid_hip -> neck` on a forward-leaning runner, giving `1.78104 units/px`) diverges by more than 15% from the rig-to-pose leg-length ratio (`485.09 / 399.28 = 1.215 units/px`) or hip-width ratio (`84.0 / 82.5 = 1.018 units/px`), `rig-pose retarget` prints `SCALE 1.78104 rig units per picture px` with no warning that the chosen `translate.scale` segment is foreshortened relative to the legs and hips. Printing the leg-length and hip-width scale ratios beside `SCALE` (and warning when `SCALE` lies outside `0.85–1.15×` of them) would immediately point the author to an unforeshortened segment like `r_shoulder -> l_shoulder`.
4. **`@rig-c/parts` / `rig-pose retarget` (`SHEAR` diagnostic on feet and shins):** `@rig-c/parts` proposes `foot_r`/`foot_l` with `"inherit": "noRotationOrReflection"` and `leg_r1`/`leg_l1` with default `"normal"` inheritance. As soon as `--leg-scale` is used in `rig-pose retarget`, `foot_r`/`foot_l` inherit the shin's `0.5×` `scalex` and squash vertically to half height (`SHEAR` up to `30.93°`). Either `@rig-c/parts` should default level feet to `"inherit": "onlyTranslation"` and lower limb links to `"inherit": "noScale"`, or `rig-pose retarget`'s `SHEAR` line should explicitly name `"inherit": "noScale"` / `"onlyTranslation"` as the remedy.
