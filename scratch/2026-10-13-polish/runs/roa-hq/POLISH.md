# Polish loop — the dancer (owner's method, 2026-10-13)

Compare the rig against the reference video **one unit at a time**, every pass. Each finding is one of:
**fix the rig**, **fix the motion**, **paint a new part/angle** (a new set, or a set at another angle), or **a tool card**
(could pose/parts/core have done it from their inputs? → card). Then loop again.

| unit | what to compare with the video |
| --- | --- |
| head | expression, blinks, head tilt |
| arms | arm angle, joint angle, hand shape, **in front of / behind** the body |
| body | breathing; jumping or bobbing; torso sway |
| legs | feet planted steadily on the floor; landings and jumps natural; nothing passes through the legs |
| cloth | skirt: swing, flare and pleat deformation, never through the legs |
